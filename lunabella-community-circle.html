🦋 Lunabella's Community Circle

It takes a village, and we're building our own.

Whether you're autistic yourself, supporting a loved one, raising a child, or simply looking for people who understand, you belong here.

Share your journey, ask questions, celebrate milestones, talk about challenges, or simply read and listen. If you'd like to talk about your own diagnosis or a loved one's diagnosis, you're welcome to—but no diagnosis, medical records, or personal medical information are required.

There's no pressure to post. You can take your time, find common ground, and learn from others with similar experiences. Every journey is different, and every voice matters.

All Are Welcome. All Are Included. All Are Equal.

💬 What can we talk about?

🛡️ Development & Safety

Everyday safety, planning, and family experiences.

💬 Communication & AAC

Communication differences, tools, and progress.

🧩 Sensory Support

Sensory needs, comfort, and strategies that help.

🌈 Routines & Daily Life

Transitions, schedules, and the little things that matter.

💜 Family & Caregiver Support

Understanding, encouragement, and being heard.

⭐ Wins & Celebrations

Share milestones, joy, and proud moments.

💜 Join the conversation

We are preparing a way for visitors to create posts and reply to one another. Posting isn't available yet. We won't ask you to submit anything until the conversation system and safeguards are ready.

For everyone's privacy, please don't share children's names alongside sensitive medical details, addresses, school information, or identifying records in public conversations. Respect others' choices about what they share.

Come as you are. Talk to us. Let's become a community.
