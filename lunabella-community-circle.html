💜 Welcome to Our Village!

A personal message from Samuel about why we built this space—and why you belong here.

💌 Read Samuel’s Welcome ⌄

First, I want to personally thank each and every one of you for stopping by and visiting our little corner of STASHUNARY.

As parents, we know that raising and supporting our children comes with beautiful moments, challenges, questions, and experiences that not everybody understands.

That's why we created Lunabella's Community Circle.

We wanted to build a place where parents, caregivers, autistic individuals, families, and supporters can come together, share experiences, ask questions, exchange ideas, celebrate accomplishments, and learn from one another.

You don't have to have all the answers. You don't have to share your diagnosis or personal information. You don't even have to post anything if you're not comfortable. Sometimes just knowing somebody else understands what you're going through can make a world of difference.

We ask only that everyone treat each other with kindness, understanding, and respect.

It takes a village, and we're building our own. 💜

Thank you for being part of ours.

Samuel (Owner)Lunabella's World | STASHUNARY
