Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy describes how Milestone Brands LLC (“we,” “us”) handles personal information through STASHUNARY and its linked services, including digital products, games, educational resources, creative services and customer support. Separate product policies may provide additional details; where a product-specific policy applies, read it together with this policy.

Information we collect

Depending on the service you use, we may receive your name, email address, account or player nickname, purchases and order references, messages, custom requests, files and content you choose to submit, and gameplay or service activity needed to provide the experience. Website and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, cookies, local storage, and security or usage logs. Do not send Social Security numbers, passwords or complete payment-card information in forms or messages.

How information is used

We use information to provide purchased products and services, create or deliver requested materials, maintain accounts and game access, respond to support inquiries, prevent misuse, resolve disputes, improve reliability and comply with applicable obligations. We may send transactional messages about your order or account. Any optional promotional communications should provide a way to opt out.

Payments and service providers

Payment providers such as Stripe handle checkout and payment information. We may receive payment status, transaction identifiers and limited billing details, but do not need your full payment-card number. Our websites and individual products may use third-party hosting, storage, authentication, analytics, email, or digital-delivery services; those providers handle information as needed to supply their services. Some purchases or digital products may be hosted or delivered by external platforms, whose own policies also apply.

Games and community features

Some games may store player names, room information, purchase entitlements, answers, scores and session activity. If you join a multiplayer room or share results, other participants or recipients may see information you choose to display. Avoid including sensitive personal details in nicknames, chat, responses or shared content. Adult-marked games are intended only for adults aged 18 or older.

Children and educational content

STASHUNARY offers family and educational content. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use and avoid submitting unnecessary personal information about a child. We do not intend adult games for children. If you believe a child provided personal information that should be removed, contact us. Before introducing accounts, leaderboards or other features that collect personal information directly from children under 13, additional parental-consent and privacy controls may be required.

Creative services and uploads

When you request custom cards, documents or other personalized products, we may process the text, images and files you submit to fulfill your order. Do not upload material you lack permission to share. The separate Resume Studio Privacy Policy provides additional details for résumé orders and uploaded career documents.

Cookies, retention and security

Browser storage, cookies and similar technologies may support site operation, sessions, preferences and security. Retention periods vary by service, provider, legal requirements and operational needs; we do not promise automatic deletion on a fixed schedule. We use reasonable safeguards and access restrictions, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information associated with you. We may need to verify your request and may retain information required for transactions, security, disputes or legal compliance. You may also manage cookies or local storage through your browser and exercise any rights provided by applicable privacy law.

Sharing and changes

We do not sell customer-submitted documents or personal information as a business product. We may disclose information to service providers, when required by law, to protect users and services, or in a business transfer subject to applicable obligations. We may update this policy as services change; revisions will be posted here with an updated effective date.
